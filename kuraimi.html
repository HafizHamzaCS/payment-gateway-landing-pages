The problem

Kuraimi Bank moves Yemen's money. But not through WooCommerce — until now.

There's no official Kuraimi E-Pay plugin for WooCommerce. Stores either take payments manually over chat, or pay a developer for a custom integration that takes weeks and breaks on the next WooCommerce update. This plugin is that integration — built, maintained, and ready to install.

How it works

Your customer pays in seconds.

1

Customer picks Kuraimi

At checkout they select "Kuraimi E-Pay" — visible on block and classic checkouts.

2

Enters PIN

They verify with their Kuraimi PIN. Rate-limited and bound to the order total.

3

Order marked paid

Payment confirms with the bank and WooCommerce marks the order paid. You ship.

4

Refund if needed

One click on the order screen triggers the bank reversal. No phone calls.

Security

Bank-grade habits, not afterthoughts.

TLS enforced in live mode

Live traffic only talks to the bank over verified HTTPS. Certificate verification is never disabled in production.

Rate-limited PIN checks

Verification endpoints are throttled, blocking brute-force PIN guessing and automated abuse.

Secrets never hit the logs

PINs, passwords and tokens are redacted before anything is written to the WooCommerce logs.

Verified amount = charged amount

Each verification is bound to the cart hash and total, and re-checked at payment time — no amount swapping mid-checkout.
